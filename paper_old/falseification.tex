    \item \textbf{Neutrino Mass Ordering:} Because neutrinos are inverse lattice resonances ($1/\Delta^n$), the system fills volumetric resonances from the ground state upward, mandating a normal hierarchy ($m_1 < m_2 < m_3$). An inverted hierarchy ($m_3 < m_1$) is geometrically impossible under this framework because it would require higher-order lattice excitations to exist without the foundational support of lower-order tiers. The confirmation of an inverted mass hierarchy by JUNO or DUNE would decisively falsify the model.
