\begin{abstract}
We develop a conditional routing model of particle decay on the proposed $E_8$ persistence substrate. Allowed transitions preserve specified labels while lowering a latency measure. A separate constant-hazard assumption supplies exponential survival and distinguishes mean lifetimes from half-lives. We state the remaining internal bridge needed for a fifth-power width law and show that lattice norm symmetry alone does not select that exponent. The standard effective weak-interaction calculation is retained as a comparison target rather than a substrate derivation. Lean checks the routing, survival, channel-sum and scaling arguments. The illustrative lifetime estimates have nonuniform accuracy; microscopic amplitudes, independently justified channel weights and controlled uncertainties remain necessary for a predictive physical theory.
\end{abstract}
